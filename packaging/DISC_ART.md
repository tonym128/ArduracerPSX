# ARDURACER PSX - DISC SURFACE SILK-SCREEN ARTWORK

**Media Specification:**
- **Disc Format:** Standard 120mm Compact Disc
- **Print Method:** 3-Color Silk-Screen on Metallic Polycarbonate Surface
- **Colors:**
  1. Base: Mirror Silver Polycarbonate (Disc Substrate)
  2. Ink 1: Crimson Red (`Pantone 186 C`)
  3. Ink 2: Opaque Matte Black (`Pantone Black 6 C`)
  4. Ink 3: Warm Canary Yellow (`Pantone 109 C`)

### Dimensions:
- **Outer Diameter:** 120.0 mm
- **Inner Stacking Ring:** 33.0 mm (non-printed)
- **Inner Mirror Band Boundary:** 46.0 mm (clear transition)
- **Active Silk-Screen Area:** 46.0 mm to 117.0 mm

## 2. Graphic Elements & Typography

1. **Upper Quadrant:**
   - Official PlayStation Logo (Black outline with clear inner fill).
   - "COMPACT DISC" standard licensing emblem.
2. **Horizontal Center Band:**
   - Stylized bold italicized **ARDURACER PSX** logotype printed in vibrant Crimson Red with a Matte Black drop-shadow.
   - Dual speed chevrons flanking the title.
3. **Left Lower Quadrant:**
   - Product Code: `SLUS-01995`
   - Audio Standard: `COMPACT DISC DIGITAL AUDIO` (7 Redbook Audio Tracks)
   - Copyright notice: `(C) 2026 PSoXide Team. All rights reserved.`
4. **Right Lower Quadrant:**
   - ESRB "E - Everyone" rating box (solid black with white text).
   - PSoXide bare-metal engine emblem.
