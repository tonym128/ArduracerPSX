## 3. High-Octane Driving Mechanics

### Drift Physics & Counter-Steering
Entering corners at high speed requires mastering the art of the drift:
1. **Initiate:** Approach the corner at speed, steer sharply into the apex, and tap the **Handbrake (R1 / Circle)**.
2. **Hold & Balance:** Once the rear tires break traction, counter-steer in the opposite direction of the turn to balance the slip angle.
3. **Avoid Spinout:** Steering too sharply into the slide without counter-steering will cause the vehicle to spin out, scrubbing all momentum.

### Dual-Tier Turbo Boost
Maintaining a controlled drift charges the vehicle's kinetic supercharger:
- **Tier 1 (Mini-Turbo - 60 ticks):** Emits blue exhaust flames and grants a +25% acceleration surge.
- **Tier 2 (Super-Turbo - 120 ticks):** Emits blazing neon orange flames, unlocking maximum top-speed overdrive for 3 full seconds!

### Surface Friction & Track Elements
- **Tarmac (Road):** 100% grip and standard acceleration.
- **Rumble Strips (Curbs):** High-frequency tactile vibration on DualShock controllers.
- **Off-Road (Grass / Dirt):** Decreases top speed by 40% and reduces steering responsiveness.
- **Barriers / Walls:** Heavy impacts trigger kinetic rebound and speed loss.

## 4. Game Modes

### 1. Grand Prix Championship
Compete against 5 relentless AI rival drivers across 4 Championship Cups:
- **Bronze Cup (Tracks 01–06):** Technical suburban circuits and beginner chicanes.
- **Silver Cup (Tracks 07–12):** Coastal highways and high-speed sweeping bends.
- **Gold Cup (Tracks 13–18):** Tight neon-drenched cyber city grids.
- **Platinum Cup (Tracks 19–24):** Mountain canyon hairpins and dev-class endurance tracks.

#### Points Standings
Points are awarded after each stage according to finish position:
- **1st Place:** 10 Points
- **2nd Place:** 6 Points
- **3rd Place:** 4 Points
- **4th Place:** 3 Points
- **5th Place:** 2 Points
- **6th Place:** 1 Point

### 2. Time Trial & Ghost Playback
Race against the clock to earn Bronze, Silver, Gold, and elusive Dev-Platinum medals!
- Save your best telemetry lap to the **Memory Card**.
- Race against a real-time semi-transparent **Ghost Car** replaying your exact line with sub-tick interpolation.

### 3. Tuning Garage
Distribute a pool of **20 Tuning Points** across 5 vehicle attributes:
- **Top Speed:** Increases terminal velocity (up to +30%).
- **Acceleration:** Enhances low-end torque and 0–60 mph sprint times.
- **Handling:** Sharpens turn-in response and reduces understeer.
- **Drift Stability:** Expands slip angle tolerance before spinout.
- **Gearing:** Balances short acceleration ratios vs tall top-end cruising.

## 6. Memory Card Management

Arduracer PSX uses **1 Block** on any PlayStation Memory Card in Slot 1 or Slot 2 (`BAS-ARDURACER-01`).
- **Persisted Data:** Championship progression, garage tuning presets, best lap records for all 24 tracks, earned medals, and 30Hz compressed ghost telemetry.
- **Animated BIOS Icon:** A custom 3-frame 16-color spinning checkered flag and race car displays in the PlayStation BIOS Memory Card Manager screen.
