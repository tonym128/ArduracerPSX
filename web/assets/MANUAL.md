## 🏎️ Controller Operations

### Classic Arcade Layout (Default)
* **D-Pad / Left Stick**: Steer Left / Right, Menu Navigation
* **✖ Cross Button**: Throttle (Accelerate) / Menu Confirm
* **■ Square Button**: Foot Brake & Reverse / Menu Back
* **● Circle Button**: Handbrake (Initiate Drift)
* **▲ Triangle Button / R1**: Engage Nitro Turbo Boost
* **START Button**: Pause Race / Resume
* **SELECT Button**: Reset / Switch Input Profile

### DualShock® Analog Controller
Ensure the red **ANALOG** LED is illuminated. 
* **Left Stick**: 360° Non-Linear Analog Steering (40% linear center + 60% cubic progression curve)
* **Right Stick (Dual Analog Mode)**: Up for Throttle, Down for Brake

## 🏁 Racing Techniques & Physics

### 1. The Power Slide (Drift)
When approaching a sharp bend:
1. Turn hard into the corner.
2. Tap the **● Circle (Handbrake)** button briefly to break rear traction.
3. Once the rear begins stepping out, apply **Counter-Steer** in the opposite direction while pinning the throttle (**✖ Cross**).
4. The slip angle will lock in, allowing your car to power through the corner at peak RPM!

### 2. Nitro Management
Your nitro reservoir is located on the right side of the HUD.
* Holding **▲ Triangle** discharges nitro into the engine, dramatically raising top speed and acceleration.
* Nitro automatically recharges when driving smoothly on regular tarmac road surfaces.

### 3. Track Hazards & Surfaces
* **Tarmac Road**: 100% Grip, full acceleration.
* **Red/White Rumble Curbs**: Slight vibration and rumble; great for cutting corners without scrubbing excessive speed.
* **Off-Road Grass/Gravel**: Reduces vehicle velocity to 35% with reduced lateral hold.
* **Oil Slicks**: Instantly reduces tire friction to zero, resulting in a 360° spin!
* **Turbo Chevron Pads**: Instantly hurls your vehicle forward with maximum turbo propulsion.
* **Armco Barriers**: Kinetic collision absorbs momentum; avoid direct head-on impacts.

## 🔧 Garage Tuning Guide

You are given **20 Tuning Points** to distribute in the Garage before entering competitions:
* **Top Speed**: Increases terminal velocity along long straights.
* **Acceleration**: Increases engine torque and 0–100 km/h sprint times out of low-speed corners.
* **Grip**: Increases lateral tire hold and reduces unintended sliding.
* **Boost**: Increases nitro boost velocity multiplier and tank discharge rate.

## 💾 Memory Card Saving

Arduracer requires **1 Block** on a PlayStation Memory Card (Slot 1).
* **Saved Data**: Championship cup progress, Gold/Silver/Bronze medals, unlocked bonus tracks, best lap times, and garage vehicle tuning setups.
* Saves are automatically verified with a 16-bit CRC checksum.
